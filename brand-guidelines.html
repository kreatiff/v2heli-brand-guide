01 / Brand overview

Brand overview

This section defines the core identity of V2 Helicopters, capturing its history, values and the strategic intent behind the brand.

Who we are

V2 Helicopters is a Brisbane-based premium aviation provider delivering modern, reliable helicopter services across flight training, scenic experiences and bespoke charter operations. Backed by decades of expertise, we combine innovation with proven operational excellence to shape a trusted and forward-looking brand.

Mission and vision

V2 Helicopters exists to deliver premium, innovative helicopter services and pilot training built on safety, precision and continual improvement. Our vision is to set a trusted benchmark for modern aviation excellence, shaping confident pilots and exceptional flight experiences across Australia.

03 / Logo

Logo

The V2 mark pairs a sheared V and 2 into one angular form. Two lockups and a standalone mark cover almost every use case.

Primary logo

The main logo uses a stacked, centred lockup, creating a balanced and refined layout. Use this format in most applications where the layout permits.

Horizontal logo

Ideal for wide formats: website navigation bars, aircraft fuselage decals, long signage and other elongated assets.

Logomark

The V2 mark on its own, for favicons, app icons and social avatars where the full wordmark will not read at small sizes.

Clear space

Minimum clear space around the logo preserves its impact, keeps it readable and stops other elements crowding it. Keep this zone free on every application.

x is the cap height of the HELICOPTERS wordmark. Keep at least 2x of clear space on every side, for both lockups.

Logo on colour

The lockup holds up on white, charcoal and lime. Keep it on solid, high-contrast grounds and avoid busy photography or low-contrast colour behind it.

Suggested minimum size: 32px tall for the stacked mark, 120px wide for the horizontal lockup. Below that, drop to the logomark alone.

05 / Typography

Brand fonts

The wordmark is set in a custom-cut display face: bold, angular and sheared to match the diagonal edge of the V2 mark. It exists only as the letterforms drawn for this lockup, not as a working font, so it is built for the logotype only and should be used elsewhere very sparingly. Every other application, headlines, body copy and UI, runs on Space Grotesk, a geometric sans with enough personality in its details to stay distinctive at small sizes without sacrificing legibility.

Display face, logotype only

06 / Voice and tone

Voice and tone

The personality traits on this page are not just adjectives for a mood board. They set the tone for everything V2 Helicopters writes, whether it's a charter quote, a training brochure or a scenic flight ad, so the voice needs to flex by audience without losing its character.

Do

  • Write like a pilot briefing a passenger: clear, confident and calm.
  • Lead with the fact, then the benefit, for charter and training copy.
  • For scenic and leisure copy, let the moment carry the weight: the view, the light, the route.
  • Use precise, real detail everywhere: flight times, aircraft models, locations, even when the copy is about the experience.

Don't

  • Don't oversell with hype words like "amazing" or "incredible", scenic flights included.
  • Don't bury the point in long, decorative sentences.
  • Don't use jokey or casual language for safety-related content.
  • Don't let scenic copy get so poetic it drops the practical detail: duration, price, what's included.
